### About

Junior Android Developer (Kotlin, Jetpack Compose) and Flutter cross-platform developer. 12+ shipped apps across solo work, teams, and freelance contracts — including a freelance delivery app published on Google Play. Building with Clean Architecture and MVVM/MVI, and lately pulling AI into the apps themselves (Gemini RAG pipelines, Groq/Whisper voice interfaces) instead of bolting a chatbot onto the side.

Currently a few months into a 9-month native mobile development diploma at ITI, on top of a Computer Science degree from Alexandria University.
